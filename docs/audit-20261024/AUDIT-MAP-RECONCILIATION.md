### Why 68 routes and 52 human screens are both valid inventory counts

`scripts/product-map-simple.mjs:509–513` explicitly skips entries without a resolved source file or outside its web-source set. The raw-only 16 are: `/*`, `/ads/launch/*`, `/bot-training`, eight `/dev/*` routes, `/hot-clients`, `/l/:token`, `/login`, `/o/:token`, `/properties/new`. They include redirects, developer routes and wrappers/unresolved route elements. They are **not sixteen disappeared production screens**, and their omission from the human list is **not behavioral proof** that public links/login/new property work. `audit-evidence-index.json.otherRouteDefinitions` retains every identity as pending.

### Why scheduler/job totals differ

Raw `product-map.mjs` collects string-literal `scheduleTracked(id, expression, ...)` using regex and only literal `JobHandler.type` in its bounded pattern. `product-flows.mjs` parses schedule arguments and exported handler objects separately. The four flow-only schedules are **interview-invite-call, live-calls-sweep, post-showing-call, seller-probe-call**. For example, live-calls-sweep's schedule expression is an `API_PROCESS_TICKS[...]` reference rather than a quoted expression. These are parser-discovery differences, not audit-created cron jobs.

The eight flow-only job identifiers are **photoPostPublishJob, sellerFactsJob, sellerPhotoInsightJob, socialRepostPublishJob, socialVideoPublishJob, tiktokPublishRetryJob, videoHandoffPublishJob, videoOpeningJob**. Their handler types use constants, so the flow generator can fall back to export names. These identifiers must not be misrepresented as eight new or missing runtime queue type strings. The complete declaration identity/file list remains in the index, with individual execution pending.

Flow totals also include **3,658 extracted branch nodes**. The human map's `translatedShare=86` concerns text translation, not test coverage. Neither value establishes exercised branches or accepted business outcomes. Generated maps themselves document limits around route order, effective permissions, database schemas, transaction boundaries, external errors and bounded expansion depth.
