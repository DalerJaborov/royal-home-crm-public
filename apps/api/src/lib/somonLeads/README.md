# Somon owner leads

Monitoring is disabled by default. A paginated ID-only baseline must finish before any advertisement can be sent. Later first observations are not asserted to be new publications. A listing passes automatically only with a non-business profile, no intermediary markers in the profile or text (negations such as «без посредников» are owner signals; «комиссия» is an agency marker), exactly one advert by the same author in «Квартиры, продажа, Душанбе» as counted from our own listing scans (baseline included; author ID stored only as a keyed HMAC), at most five total active adverts on the site, matching category and Dushanbe, and a usable telephone that is not a staff number and has not appeared in three or more different adverts within 30 days. An explicit owner claim is not required. When the total number of ads is greater than two, a complete seller profile and individual categories are checked incrementally under the shared rate limit; more than two real-estate ads reject the listing. Incomplete or unsupported profiles are held for manual review. The count is per author: records without an author key do not affect other authors. An advert whose own author is unknown is deferred (SELLER_UNKNOWN), requeued if the author appears in a later scan, and expires after three days. Records created before the author key existed receive it through a separate rekey pass over the listing (no baseline restart, delivery continues); while that pass runs, only authors with a single advert on the whole site pass, others are deferred and requeued when it finishes. Known overcount (not fixed): old baseline records stay counted. These signals do not prove ownership.

Source requests use a database-backed budget and lease. 403 stops the source; 429 and network/5xx errors pause it. The listing scan has a durable cursor and warns if the coverage limit or a backlog is reached. The Telegram send is reserved atomically and fenced against stop/recipient changes; an unconfirmed result is never automatically replayed. Contact fingerprints are keyed and phone values/photos are not stored. Accepted receipts expire; minimal seen IDs and contact fingerprints are retained to suppress duplicate outreach. The keyed fingerprints (`phoneHash` in receipts/sightings and `sellerKey` on seen adverts) depend on `SETTINGS_ENCRYPTION_KEY`: rotating the key silently breaks repeat-contact matching and per-author counts, so key rotation requires an explicit migration/disable plan before use.

Targeted tests cover access control, source leases, baseline, provider errors and concurrency on an isolated database. Exact original publication times and ownership cannot be established reliably from the observed source fields. Categories may change during inspection, source counts can be inconsistent, and large or incomplete profiles require manual review. Full official CRM release gates and separate production acceptance are required for changes.
